## Complementary responsibilities
- Stream Inspector: offline SSE/NDJSON framing, text reconstruction, and final tool-call JSON inspection.
- Retry Safety Lab: offline layered-retry, duplicate-side-effect, and idempotency-TTL simulations.
- CORS & Cookie Doctor: browser CORS, preflight, credentials, and cookie-policy diagnostics.

Every app includes direct navigation to both sibling apps and the owner-supplied central hub. Navigation never transfers captures or scenario inputs. The hub repository was not accessible at provisioning time and is not created or changed as part of this three-app batch.
